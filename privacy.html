Privacy Policy for MewMoo

Last updated: September 15, 2026

MewMoo is a children's educational and entertainment application designed to provide interactive animal sounds, animations, vehicles, birds, aquatic creatures, and other child-friendly learning experiences.

We take children's privacy seriously and have designed MewMoo to minimize the collection and use of personal information.

1. Information We Collect

MewMoo does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the app.

MewMoo does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Microphone recordings
  • Camera data

MewMoo does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

2. Children's Privacy

MewMoo is designed for children and families. We do not require children to provide personal information to access the app's main content.

MewMoo does not use children's personal information for advertising, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

3. Advertising

MewMoo currently does not display third-party advertisements.

We do not use advertising identifiers to provide personalized advertising to children.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before such functionality is introduced.

4. In-App Purchases

MewMoo may offer optional purchases such as:

  • Full app unlocks
  • Animal packs
  • Bird packs
  • Vehicle packs
  • Other additional content

Purchases made through the Android version of MewMoo are processed using Google Play's billing system.

MewMoo does not directly collect, process, or store users' credit card, debit card, UPI, banking, or other payment credentials.

The app may receive information necessary to determine whether a purchase was successfully completed, such as a product identifier, purchase status, or purchase token.

5. Restoring Purchases

MewMoo may communicate with Google Play to determine whether the Google Play account currently using the device has previously purchased content.

This allows previously purchased content to be restored without requiring the user to purchase it again.

6. Information Stored on the Device

MewMoo may store application settings and preferences locally on the user's device.

This may include:

  • App preferences
  • Sound settings
  • Selected categories
  • Content unlock status
  • Other settings necessary for the app to function

7. Analytics and Tracking

MewMoo currently does not use behavioral advertising or user-profiling systems.

We do not sell children's personal information.

If analytics, crash-reporting, or similar services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Device Permissions

MewMoo only requests permissions necessary for the functionality of the app.

The app does not currently require access to:

  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Call history
  • SMS messages
  • Camera
  • Microphone

9. Third-Party Services

MewMoo may use Google services necessary for distributing and operating the Android application, including Google Play and Google Play Billing.

These services are operated by Google and may process information according to Google's own privacy policies.

10. Data Sharing

MewMoo does not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be processed by platform providers when necessary to provide services such as application distribution and in-app purchases.

11. Data Retention

MewMoo aims to collect and retain as little user information as possible.

Information stored locally by MewMoo remains on the user's device until the app data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Purchase records maintained by Google Play are subject to Google's own data-retention practices.

12. Parental Controls and Purchases

Purchases in MewMoo are intended to be managed by a parent, guardian, or other authorized adult.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to use Google Play's parental controls and purchase-approval features where appropriate.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MewMoo's features, technologies, or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed.

14. Contact Us

If you are a parent, guardian, or user and have questions about this Privacy Policy or MewMoo's privacy practices, please contact us.
